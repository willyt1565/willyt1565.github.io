Property Management in Houston, TX

Newera Property Management manages single-family and multifamily rentals in Houston, Katy, Cypress, Spring, The Woodlands, Tomball, Sugar Land, Pearland and the surrounding area, from our office on State Highway 249. We are a licensed Texas real estate broker (TREC #9016567-BB), built by real estate investors. Owners pay 8% of monthly rent with no markup on vendor invoices, and can see rent, work orders and inspection photos in their owner portal at any time.

Where we work in the Houston area

We manage homes across Houston and in these suburbs, where single-family rentals are most common:

  • HoustonNeighborhoods across the city, managed from our Northwest Houston office on State Highway 249.
  • KatyWest along I-10, with large master-planned communities and strong demand from families.
  • CypressNorthwest along US 290, a short drive from our office, with a deep pool of single-family rentals.
  • SpringNorth along I-45 and the Grand Parkway, one of the area's largest markets for rental homes.
  • The WoodlandsA master-planned community north of Houston, where HOA rules and home upkeep standards matter.
  • TomballNorthwest along State Highway 249, minutes from our office, with newer subdivisions and growing demand.
  • Sugar LandSouthwest in Fort Bend County, with established family neighborhoods that draw long-term renters.
  • PearlandSouth along SH 288, popular with residents who commute to the Texas Medical Center.

These are the areas we work in most, but we are not limited to them. If your property is elsewhere in the Greater Houston area, ask us and we'll let you know if we can manage it.

Who looks after your property in Houston?

Newera assigns a dedicated team member to each market we serve. Your Houston-area property is handled by our Houston team member, who takes care of showings, inspections and vendor visits locally. Owners who live out of the area get scheduled check-ins, and everything is visible in the owner portal.

Managing a Houston rental: what owners should know

  • Hurricane season. Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30. When a storm causes damage, our Houston team member coordinates repairs through our vendor network, keeps you updated and documents the work with photos in your owner portal.
  • Flood risk and insurance. Many Houston-area homes sit in or near a flood zone. Keep your landlord policy and any flood coverage current. Our 6-month inspection photos give you a dated record of the home's condition.
  • HOAs and MUD taxes. Many suburbs, including Katy, Cypress, Spring and The Woodlands, have HOAs and Municipal Utility District taxes. We factor these into rent pricing and make sure residents know the HOA rules that apply to the home.
  • Heat and humidity. AC is the repair residents care about most. We prioritize AC failures by urgency and keep you informed before larger repairs.

What's included

  • Your listing live on Zillow, Trulia, Apartments.com, Realtor.com, Rent. and HotPads within 24–48 hours of the marketing agreement
  • Consistent, Fair-Housing compliant screening on every applicant, based on our published Resident Qualification Criteria
  • Online rent collection, with owner payouts on a consistent monthly schedule, typically by the 10th once rent has been collected and cleared
  • Property inspections every 6 months, documented with photos and shared in your owner portal
  • Maintenance prioritized by urgency: emergencies typically dispatched within the hour, urgent items the same day, routine items within a few business days
  • A maintenance reserve covers routine repairs under $500, replenished from rent and itemized on your statement; larger repairs reviewed with you first
  • 24/7 emergency line answered by a real person, not a call center

Questions Houston owners ask us

What areas around Houston do you serve?

We manage rentals in Houston, Katy, Cypress, Spring, The Woodlands, Tomball, Sugar Land and Pearland, and we are not limited to these areas. If your property is elsewhere in the Greater Houston area, contact us and we'll let you know if we can take it on.

Where is your Houston office?

Our Houston office is at 17350 State Hwy 249, Suite 220, Houston, TX 77064. You can reach our Houston team at (832) 558-2911.

What happens if a storm damages my Houston rental?

Residents can reach our 24/7 emergency line, and our Houston team member coordinates repairs through our vendor network. We keep you updated, review larger repairs with you first, and document the work with photos in your owner portal.

I live out of state. Can you manage my Houston rental?

Yes. Everything is visible in your owner portal: rent payments, statements, work orders, and inspection reports with photos. We keep in touch through scheduled check-ins, and we reach out whenever something needs your attention or decision.

Can I switch to Newera from my current property manager?

Yes. It starts with a free consultation, where we review your property and your current management agreement. From there, we coordinate the handover with your current manager, including leases, security deposits, keys and property records, and keep you informed at each step so the transition is as smooth as possible for you and your residents.

Do you manage multifamily properties?

Yes. We manage multifamily rentals as well as single-family homes.

How much does property management cost in Houston with Newera?

Our management fee is 8% of the monthly rent, with portfolio discounts down to as little as 6% for owners with multiple properties. Leasing and renewal fees are quoted during your free consultation, based on your property. There is no markup on vendor invoices and no administration fee.

Do you manage short-term rentals or Airbnbs?

No. We focus on long-term residential rentals: single-family and multifamily homes leased to residents on standard leases.
