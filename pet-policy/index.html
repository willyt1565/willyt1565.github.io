Pet & Animal Policy

Every applicant and resident completes a free or paid PetScreening profile, even with no pets. It keeps pet and assistance animal reviews fair and consistent for everyone.

Start Your PetScreening Profile ↗

This policy is not approval to keep a pet. No pet may live at or visit the property until Newera approves it in writing and a pet addendum is signed.

Step by Step

How to Complete Your Profile

Go to newerapropertymanagement.petscreening.com and choose the profile that fits your household. Only one adult per household needs to complete it.

  1. 1

    Choose your profile type

    No pet, household pet, or assistance animal (see below).

  2. 2

    Add your pet's details

    For each pet, upload a current photo and vaccination records.

  3. 3

    Request an accommodation

    For an assistance animal, submit your request and supporting documentation.

  4. 4

    Submit your profile

    It is shared with Newera automatically. Your application is not complete until it is submitted.

Policy Details

What to Know Before You Apply

Allowed pets

Dogs and cats are accepted at pet-friendly properties. Other animals are reviewed case by case.

  • Each property's listing states whether pets are allowed there.
  • All pets must be up to date on vaccinations.
  • Approval covers only the pets named in your pet addendum. A new or replacement pet needs its own profile and approval first.

Pet fees and deposit

Your monthly pet fee depends on your pet's FIDO Score (paw rating) from PetScreening. A higher score means lower risk and a lower fee.

  • A pet deposit applies per approved pet and is added to your security deposit.
  • The monthly pet fee is paid with rent for as long as the pet lives at the property.
  • No pet fees, pet rent or pet deposits apply to verified assistance animals.

Restricted breeds

Some breeds may not be approved because of the property owner's insurance requirements. If you are unsure whether your pet's breed is restricted, ask us before you apply.

Breed restrictions do not apply to verified assistance animals.

Assistance animals

Service and support animals are not pets, and requests are reviewed under federal Fair Housing guidelines. Submit your request through newerapropertymanagement.petscreening.com using the free assistance animal profile.

  • No PetScreening fee, pet fee, pet rent or pet deposit.
  • Pet limits and breed restrictions do not apply.
  • Residents remain responsible for any damage the animal causes.
  • Created a household pet profile by mistake? PetScreening can convert it and refund the fee after review.
Living With Pets

Pet Rules

Residents are responsible for their animals at all times.

  • Pick up pet waste in the yard regularly.
  • Keep pets leashed or contained when outside the home or yard.
  • Keep fences and gates secure.
  • Do not leave pets unattended for long periods or in a way that disturbs neighbors.
  • Residents pay for any damage, cleaning or pest treatment caused by an animal.
  • Unapproved animals at the property may result in fees and a lease violation.
  • Keep your PetScreening profile active and renew it yearly while the pet lives at the property.
