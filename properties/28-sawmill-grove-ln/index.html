Tucked beneath the tall pines of The Woodlands' original Village of Grogan's Mill, this 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhome offers roughly 1,428 square feet of comfortable living just minutes from Town Center. The first floor centers on a spacious 18x15 living room, a welcoming place to unwind at the end of the day or host friends for game night. A convenient half bath downstairs keeps guests away from the private spaces. Upstairs, all three bedrooms share the second floor in a split layout.

The primary suite includes its own en-suite bath with a walk-in shower, while the secondary bedrooms share a full bath with a tub/shower combo and vanity area. The third bedroom works equally well as a guest room, home office, or hobby space. A one-car garage adds secure parking and extra storage, and the community pool is ready for warm Texas afternoons. Step outside and you're surrounded by the wooded setting The Woodlands is known for, with shaded hike-and-bike pathways winding through the village. Nearby Sawmill Park offers a splash pad, tennis courts, sports fields, and picnic areas, and golfers can tee off at the TPC and other courses at The Woodlands Resort and Country Club, both in the village.

Everyday errands are easy at Grogan's Mill Village Center, with groceries, restaurants, and local shops close by. For a night out, head to Market Street, The Woodlands Mall, and the Waterway for shopping, dining, and waterfront strolls, or catch a concert at the Cynthia Woods Mitchell Pavilion. Hospitals and medical offices are a short drive away. Commuting is a breeze with quick access to I-45, the Hardy Toll Road, and the Grand Parkway, plus the ExxonMobil campus and Houston's Bush Intercontinental Airport within easy reach.

Served by Conroe ISD. Schedule your private showing today and see what life in Grogan's Mill has to offer.
