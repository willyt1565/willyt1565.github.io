5710 Prentiss Dr is a renovated 4-bedroom, 3-bath single-story home for sale in San Antonio's Oak Hills Terrace neighborhood (ZIP 78240). Listed at $385,000 with no HOA, this 2,372-square-foot ranch-style home sits on a 0.21-acre lot just minutes from the South Texas Medical Center, USAA, Loop 410, and I-10, and is served by Northside ISD schools.

The floor plan is open and bright, with two separate living areas that give you room to relax, work from home, or host guests. At the center of the home, the updated kitchen features granite countertops, white cabinetry, a subway tile backsplash, a breakfast bar, a stainless microwave and dishwasher, and a large walk-in pantry with floor-to-ceiling shelving. A dedicated dining area opens to the backyard.

All four bedrooms and three full bathrooms are on one level. The spacious rear bedroom opens through French doors to the patio, making it a flexible fit for a guest suite, home office, or hobby room. Recent updates include fresh interior and exterior paint, new flooring, updated lighting, and electrical improvements, so the home is ready for its next owner to move right in. An indoor utility room adds everyday convenience.

Outside, the stone-and-siding exterior, attached two-car garage, and wide driveway create strong curb appeal, while the large privacy-fenced backyard with mature trees offers plenty of space to entertain, play, or garden. If you're looking for an updated single-story home in Northwest San Antonio near the Medical Center, 5710 Prentiss Dr is worth a visit.
