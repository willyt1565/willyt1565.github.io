Now leasing — this 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom townhome offers approximately 1,527 sq. ft. of thoughtfully laid-out living space in the gated Echo Creek community in San Antonio. Two bedrooms are located on the main level, each with easy access to a full bath — great for roommates or guests. The primary suite is upstairs, offering a private retreat separate from the main living areas, complete with its own en-suite bath.

The great room is anchored by a stone, wood-burning fireplace beneath a dramatic vaulted ceiling, and opens onto a kitchen with granite counters, subway-tile backsplash, and stainless appliances. The kitchen and all three bathrooms have been recently updated, giving the home a fresh, modern feel while keeping the classic townhome charm.

The unit comes equipped with a refrigerator, range, dishwasher, and microwave, plus washer/dryer hookups so you can bring your own set and get settled in right away. Outside, enjoy a private patio behind the controlled-access gate of the Echo Creek community. Located just off Huebner and Eckhert Roads — walking distance to shopping and minutes from the South Texas Medical Center's major employers. Contact us today to schedule a showing.
