Resident Qualification Criteria

Please review our rental criteria before you apply. These standards are published in advance, as required by Texas Property Code § 92.3515, and applied the same way to every applicant without regard to any protected class.

We do not pre-screen applications. Please review the criteria below to confirm you meet the minimum requirements. If you do, you are encouraged to apply.

  • Multiple applications may be accepted for the same property. The most qualified applicant will be selected.
  • Incomplete applications will not be considered.
  • If others will be applying with you, all applications must be submitted at the same time to be considered.
  • By submitting an application, you acknowledge that you have read and understand these criteria and policies.
Download the printable Rental Criteria (PDF)
Qualification Standards

Applicants

Every Adult 18+ Applies

  • Each person 18 or older who will live in the home must complete an online application.
  • Co-signers are not accepted. The financially responsible person must reside at the property.
  • A government-issued photo ID is required.

Income

3× Monthly Rent

  • One applicant must have gross monthly income of at least 3 times the monthly rent.
  • Up to two incomes may be combined, but together must equal at least 3.5 times the monthly rent. Combined income must come from people who will live in the home.
  • Income is verified through the employer's HR department or the prior month's pay stubs showing year-to-date totals, provided with the application.
  • 25% of child support received is counted toward income.
  • Self-employed applicants must provide the 2 most recent years of tax returns, with no exceptions. The adjusted gross income (AGI) must meet the income requirement. Bank statements are used only together with tax returns.
  • Income that cannot be verified will not be considered.

Credit

640+ TransUnion Score

  • We use TransUnion for credit scores and reporting. Scores from other sources, and reports supplied by applicants, are not accepted.
  • A credit score of 640 or higher is required. Exceptions are occasionally made for scores between 550 and 639, after a complete application has been reviewed.
  • The security deposit amount is scaled based on your credit score.
  • Bankruptcy status and age determine whether an application is approved with an increased deposit or denied.

Applications will be denied for:

  • No credit score, or a report showing N/A
  • Any debt owed to the Attorney General's Office for back child support
  • An open federal tax lien

Rental History

Clean Last 5 Years

  • An eviction, broken lease, or judgment within the last 5 years results in automatic denial.
  • An eviction or balance owed to a previous landlord that is more than 5 years old may result in an increased deposit or denial.
  • Rental verifications from family members are not accepted.
  • Please provide correct contact information for each previous landlord.

Criminal Background

A criminal background check is run on every applicant. Felony convictions involving persons or property are cause for automatic rejection. Submitting an application authorizes Newera Property Management to perform a criminal background check.

Fees & Move-In Requirements

Application fee

Per adult applicant. Covers the credit and background check. Non-refundable.

$75

Lease administration fee

Charged at lease signing and again at each renewal.

$49/mo

Resident Benefits Package

Required with all Newera lease agreements and billed monthly.

$100K

Renters insurance

Minimum liability coverage, required before move-in.

Renters insurance requirements

Proof of the following must be provided to Newera Property Management before your move-in date:

  • Evidence of the required coverage, showing the policy is in effect, its start and end dates, and who is named on the policy.
  • Newera Property Management named as an "Additional Interested" party on the insurance binder.
  • Resident liability coverage of at least $100,000.
Applicant Rights & Policies
01

Non-Discrimination Policy

Newera Property Management does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability, familial status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or veteran status. San Antonio and Houston Fair Housing Ordinance protections are fully honored.

02

Adverse Action Notice

If an application is denied in whole or in part based on information from a consumer report, applicants will receive a written adverse action notice per FCRA requirements, including the consumer reporting agency's name and a statement of applicant rights.

03

Accommodation Requests

Applicants with disabilities may request a reasonable accommodation in the application or screening process. Requests should be submitted in writing to our office prior to application review.

Acknowledgment of Tenant Selection Criteria

As required by Texas Property Code § 92.3515, these tenant selection criteria and the grounds on which an application may be denied are provided to every applicant. Grounds include criminal history, previous rental history, current income, credit history, and inaccurate or incomplete information on the application. If you do not meet the criteria, or provide inaccurate or incomplete information, your application may be denied and the application fee will not be refunded. Each applicant 18 or older signs the acknowledgment included in the printable Rental Criteria.
